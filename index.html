Cabin Assistance Interface Study

You will complete a general cabin-crew assistance task using two simulated aircraft interfaces.

The order of the interfaces is assigned automatically. No real cabin crew will be contacted.

Please participate only after reading the research information and providing consent through the researcher's approved procedure.



Use the same participant ID in Microsoft Forms. Do not enter your name or email address.
